# TechNova - Sample Employee Handbook

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026

## General Employment Policies

TechNova Solutions is a technology company specializing in software development, artificial intelligence, cloud computing, and IT consulting.

All employees are expected to maintain professional conduct, respect colleagues, and comply with company policies.

The standard working schedule is Monday to Friday, from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, including a one-hour lunch break.

Employees must notify their immediate manager if they expect to arrive late or cannot attend work.

Repeated lateness or unexplained absences may result in disciplinary action.

New employees must complete a three-month probation period during which their performance and suitability for the role are evaluated.

## Remote Work Policy

Eligible employees may work remotely for up to two days per week, subject to manager approval.

Remote employees must remain available during normal working hours and attend all scheduled meetings.

Employees working remotely are responsible for maintaining a reliable internet connection and protecting confidential company information.

Working remotely from another country requires written approval from HR and management.

## Leave Policies

Full-time employees receive 25 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year.

Annual leave requests must be submitted at least 14 calendar days in advance and require manager approval.

Employees may carry forward up to five unused annual leave days, subject to management approval.

Employees unable to work due to illness must inform their manager and HR as soon as reasonably possible.

A medical certificate is required for sick leave lasting more than two consecutive working days.

Parental leave is provided to eligible employees according to their employment contracts and applicable employment legislation.

Employees planning parental leave should contact HR to discuss documentation, entitlements, and arrangements.

The company observes official public holidays applicable to its operating jurisdiction.

Employees required to work during public holidays must obtain manager approval and will receive applicable compensation or alternative leave.

## Compensation and Benefits

Employee salaries are paid monthly on the last working day of each month through direct bank transfer.

Employees must ensure that their registered banking information is accurate and up to date.

Salary discrepancies, missing payments, or unexplained deductions should be reported to the Payroll Department.

Employees must receive written manager approval before performing overtime work.

Approved overtime compensation is calculated according to employment contracts and applicable legislation.

Eligible full-time employees receive an annual professional development allowance of $1,000.

The training allowance can be used for approved certifications, technical courses, workshops, and industry conferences.

Employees must obtain manager approval before purchasing training materials or registering for reimbursable courses.

Business-related expenses can be reimbursed when they have a legitimate business purpose and prior authorization.

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 calendar days and include itemized receipts and manager approval.

The Finance Department processes approved reimbursement claims within 15 working days.

## Performance and Professional Development

Formal employee performance reviews take place twice per year.

Performance evaluations consider work quality, achievement of objectives, communication, teamwork, problem-solving, and reliability.

Managers discuss employee achievements, areas for improvement, and future objectives during individual review meetings.

Employees who disagree with their performance evaluation may submit a written response to HR within seven working days.

TechNova Solutions encourages continuous learning through professional certifications, training courses, and industry events.

Employees are encouraged to discuss career development goals with their managers.

## Workplace Conduct

All employees must demonstrate honesty, professionalism, accountability, and respect toward colleagues and customers.

Harassment, discrimination, bullying, intimidation, and workplace violence are strictly prohibited.

Employees who experience or witness harassment should report the incident to HR.

Complaints will be investigated fairly and handled confidentially to the extent reasonably possible.

Retaliation against employees who report misconduct in good faith is prohibited.

The company follows a business-casual dress code, requiring employees to wear clean and professional clothing.

Formal business attire may be required during client meetings, conferences, and external business events.

The company provides reasonable accommodations for cultural, religious, and disability-related needs.

## IT and Technology Policies

Company computers, software, email accounts, and networks must primarily be used for authorized business activities.

Employees must install only approved software and avoid downloading suspicious files.

Company devices must be locked whenever they are left unattended.

Lost or stolen company devices must be reported to the IT Department immediately.

Employees must use strong and unique passwords containing at least 12 characters.

Passwords must never be shared with colleagues or external individuals.

Multi-factor authentication must be enabled wherever supported by company systems.

Employees must immediately report suspicious login notifications, phishing attempts, or suspected account compromises to the IT Security Team.

The IT Department provides technical assistance, password resets, and account recovery support.

## Data Privacy and Confidentiality

Employees may have access to confidential company, customer, and employee information during their employment.

Confidential information includes customer records, financial reports, employee information, source code, and internal business documents.

Employees must only access information necessary for their assigned job responsibilities.

Confidential information must not be shared with unauthorized individuals or external organizations.

Suspected data breaches and accidental information disclosures must be reported immediately to the IT Security Team.

Employees remain responsible for protecting confidential information after their employment ends, subject to applicable law.

## Resignation and Termination

Employees wishing to resign must submit a formal written resignation to their manager and HR.

The standard contractual notice period is 30 calendar days unless otherwise specified by the employment contract or applicable legislation.

Employees are expected to continue performing their responsibilities professionally throughout the notice period.

Before leaving, employees must return company property and transfer relevant work responsibilities.

HR coordinates final salary calculations, outstanding leave balances, and employment documentation.

## Employee Support

Employees may contact the Human Resources Department for questions concerning employment contracts, leave entitlements, workplace complaints, or company policies.

The Payroll Department handles salary payments, deductions, and compensation-related enquiries.

The IT Department handles technical issues, company devices, account access, and cybersecurity incidents.

The Finance Department handles approved business expense claims and reimbursement enquiries.

Employees should initially contact their immediate manager when they require clarification about departmental procedures.
